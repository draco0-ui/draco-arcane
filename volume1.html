COURSE OVERVIEW

Master Modern Card Magic

Volume I is a premium intermediate course created for magicians who want to improve their technical skills, presentation, audience management, and performance confidence. Instead of teaching isolated tricks, this course focuses on building complete routines, natural handling, and the mindset required to perform modern card magic professionally. Every lesson is carefully structured so that each technique supports the next, allowing you to develop a strong foundation for future Draco Arcane volumes.
